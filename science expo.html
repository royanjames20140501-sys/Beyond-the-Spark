01 — WELCOME TO BEYOND THE SPARK

What is ionic wind?

Ionic wind is airflow set in motion by electrical forces. In an electrohydrodynamic (EHD) generator, a high electric field ionizes a small region of air. The charged particles move, collide with neutral molecules, and entrain the surrounding gas.

01 / IONS

Air with a charge

An ion is an atom or molecule with an electric charge: it has gained or lost electrons.

02 / EHD

Electricity meets fluid

EHD is what happens when electric fields push on charged particles in a fluid.

03 / IONIC WIND

Momentum becomes motion

Ions bump into ordinary air molecules and set them moving too.

Images show published research and demonstrations; the NASA spacecraft thruster is included as a visual comparison and operates by a different mechanism.

02 — HOW DOES IT WORK?

A current you
can’t see.

A fine wire, a curved collector and a high-voltage field. Together, they set ordinary air in motion—without a fan blade turning.

STEP 01 / FIELD

Concentrate the field

A thin wire concentrates the electric field. Nearby air molecules become charged.

STEP 02 / CORONA

Create charged ions

A faint corona glow appears as electrons move between air molecules.

STEP 03 / MOMENTUM

Move the air

The field speeds ions toward the collector. Their collisions push the surrounding air.

STEP 04 / REACTION

Feel the thrust

Air flows one way; the device feels a push the other way. That is thrust.

06 — WHAT COULD COME NEXT?

A glance into the future

Three research directions where quiet electric airflow could find a role. These are possibilities under study, not promises of ready-to-use technology.

✦〰
01 / SMALL-SCALE FLIGHT

Quiet micro-flyers

Tiny inspection craft could use distributed EHD thrusters for gentle, low-speed flight in controlled spaces. Low thrust and payload limits remain major hurdles.

RESEARCH FRONTIER
02 / NEXT-GEN COOLING

Fanless cooling layers

Thin ionic-wind modules could move air across crowded electronics without spinning blades, opening new ways to manage heat in compact devices.

DESIGN OPPORTUNITY
03 / SMART AIRFRAMES

Adaptive airflow surfaces

Embedded plasma actuators might gently reshape airflow over small aircraft surfaces, helping researchers explore control without bulky moving parts.

ACTIVE RESEARCH

EHD wind works by pushing surrounding air, so these concepts need an atmosphere. Space ion engines are a different technology.

08 — SAFETY & TAKEAWAY

Big ideas.
Safe science.

EHD is exciting to explore. High voltage and the gases from corona discharge deserve respect.

⚠
HIGH VOLTAGE + OZONE

High voltage can cause severe shock, burns or fire. Corona can produce ozone, which irritates lungs. Demonstrate only with qualified supervision, enclosed and current-limited equipment, insulation and ventilation. Keep visitors away; switch off and discharge before adjustments. Never touch energized parts or breathe near the discharge.

SCALESmall forces

Current designs produce modest thrust for their size.

POWERReal energy costs

No moving parts does not mean no power losses.
